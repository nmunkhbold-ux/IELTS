Module 16: From Band 5.5 or 6 to Band 7+

16.1 The lower-band answer (about Band 5.5)

The line graph shows the percentage of commuters who cycled to work in three cities between 2000 and 2020.

In 2000, the percentage of cyclists in Northbury is 8%, in Eastfield is 20% and in Southport is 14%. In 2005, Northbury increase to 11%, Eastfield increase to 24% and Southport decrease to 12%. In 2010, Northbury increase to 15%, Eastfield increase to 27% and Southport decrease to 9%.

In 2015, Northbury increase to 22% but Eastfield decrease to 25% and Southport stay at 9%. In 2020, Northbury increase to 28%, Eastfield decrease to 21% and Southport increase to 10%. Northbury increase the most.

It is good that more people cycle in Northbury because cycling is healthy. In conclusion, Northbury had the most cyclists and cycling is becoming more popular.

What is wrong with it (129 words).

  • It is under 150 words, which is penalised in Task Achievement.
  • The introduction copies the task word for word.
  • There is no overview. The answer reads out the figures year by year instead.
  • It uses the present tense for past dates and drops the -ed and -s endings (Northbury increase).
  • It uses increase and decrease every time, with no comparison.
  • It gives a personal opinion and a reason (it is good … because cycling is healthy) and a conclusion.
  • Every figure is accurate, which is why it is Band 5.5 and not lower.

16.2 The Band 7+ answer (178 words)

This is the model answer from Module 2.

The line graph compares the proportion of commuters who cycled to work in three cities over a twenty-year period, from 2000 to 2020.

Overall, cycling became far more popular in Northbury, whereas it declined in Southport, and Eastfield ended close to where it began after an initial rise. As a result, the city with the lowest figure in 2000 had the highest by 2020.

In 2000, Eastfield had the highest percentage of cyclists, at 20%, followed by Southport (14%) and Northbury (8%). Northbury then rose steadily, and its figure more than tripled to reach 28% in 2020, which made it the leading city. It overtook Southport between 2005 and 2010, and it passed Eastfield between 2015 and 2020.

The other two cities followed different patterns. Eastfield's figure climbed to a peak of 27% in 2010 before falling back to 21%, only one percentage point above its starting level. Southport, meanwhile, dropped from 14% to 9% by 2010, remained at that level in 2015, and then edged up to 10% in 2020, the lowest figure of the three cities.

16.3 Sentence-by-sentence comparison

Band 5.5 Band 7+ What improved
The line graph shows the percentage of commuters who cycled to work in three cities between 2000 and 2020. The line graph compares the proportion of commuters who cycled to work in three cities over a twenty-year period, from 2000 to 2020. Task Achievement and Lexis. The task is paraphrased: compares, proportion, over a twenty-year period
(No overview) Overall, cycling became far more popular in Northbury, whereas it declined in Southport, and Eastfield ended close to where it began after an initial rise. As a result, the city with the lowest figure in 2000 had the highest by 2020. Task Achievement. A clear overview, with the main trends and the change in ranking, added after the introduction
In 2000, the percentage of cyclists in Northbury is 8%, in Eastfield is 20% and in Southport is 14%. In 2000, Eastfield had the highest percentage of cyclists, at 20%, followed by Southport (14%) and Northbury (8%). Grammar and Task Achievement. Past tense, and a ranking replaces a list. Followed by and brackets save words
In 2005, Northbury increase to 11% … In 2010, Northbury increase to 15% … In 2015, Northbury increase to 22% … In 2020, Northbury increase to 28% Northbury then rose steadily, and its figure more than tripled to reach 28% in 2020, which made it the leading city. All four criteria. Four sentences become one; the verb agrees; more than tripled is a ratio; a relative clause (which made it) links the figure to its meaning
(Not mentioned) It overtook Southport between 2005 and 2010, and it passed Eastfield between 2015 and 2020. Task Achievement. The two crossings are key features, and between is accurate for data given every five years
In 2005 … Eastfield increase to 24% … In 2010 … Eastfield increase to 27% … In 2015 … Eastfield decrease to 25% … In 2020 … Eastfield decrease to 21% Eastfield's figure climbed to a peak of 27% in 2010 before falling back to 21%, only one percentage point above its starting level. Task Achievement and Lexis. The peak is the key feature, before falling is a participle clause, and percentage point is the correct unit
In 2005 … Southport decrease to 12% … In 2010 … Southport decrease to 9% … In 2015 … Southport stay at 9% … In 2020 … Southport increase to 10% Southport, meanwhile, dropped from 14% to 9% by 2010, remained at that level in 2015, and then edged up to 10% in 2020, the lowest figure of the three cities. Lexis and Grammar. Three verbs of different strength (dropped, remained, edged up), that level as a reference, and a final comparison
It is good that more people cycle in Northbury because cycling is healthy. (Deleted) Task Achievement. Opinions and reasons are not allowed
In conclusion, Northbury had the most cyclists and cycling is becoming more popular. (Deleted; the overview does this job at the start) Task Achievement and Coherence. There is no conclusion in Task 1

16.4 What changed, by criterion

Criterion Band 5.5 answer Band 7+ answer What to practise
Task Achievement No overview; every number listed; under length; an opinion and a conclusion A clear overview; selected figures; the crossings and the peak; about 178 words; no opinion Modules 13 and 14
Coherence and Cohesion One long run of similar sentences; no grouping; only in 2005, in 2010 as links Four paragraphs with one purpose each; then, meanwhile, before, as a result, whereas; it, its, that level Module 1.6 and the structure sections of Modules 2 to 8
Lexical Resource increase and decrease repeated; no comparison words rose, climbed, dropped, edged up, remained, overtook, peak, more than tripled Module 10
Grammatical Range and Accuracy Present tense for past dates; missing verb endings; only simple sentences Past simple throughout; relative clauses, participle clauses, whereas; correct agreement Module 11

16.5 Five upgrades, in order of impact

If you are at Band 6 and have limited time, make these changes in this order. The first one is usually the biggest single gain.

  1. Add a clear overview (Module 13). It is the biggest single gain.
  2. Select and group instead of listing (Module 1.7). Aim for about five to seven figures per body paragraph.
  3. Fix tense and agreement (Module 11.1 and 11.12). Read your answer once only for verb endings.
  4. Vary verbs and add comparisons (Module 10). Use a ratio, a ranking or a gap in each paragraph.
  5. Add complexity (Module 11.5 to 11.8). One whereas sentence, one relative clause and one participle clause are enough.

16.6 Band 7 self-assessment checklist

Read your answer and tick each statement that is true. These checks are teaching estimates and do not replace an examiner's marks.

Criterion Check
Task Achievement There is a clear overview with the main features and almost no figures
Every key feature is covered (the biggest change, the highest and lowest, a crossing or an exception)
All figures and units are accurate
The answer has no opinions, reasons or conclusion
It has at least 150 words
Coherence and Cohesion The introduction, the overview and two body paragraphs each have one clear purpose
The body paragraphs group information instead of listing items
A range of linking words is used naturally (not firstly, secondly everywhere)
Reference words (it, its, this, that level, respectively) avoid repetition
Lexical Resource At least five different words or phrases for change or comparison are used
The introduction paraphrases the task without changing the meaning
Words are used with the right meaning and the right grammar (rose by, peaked at, the number of)
There is no memorised or empty language
Grammatical Range and Accuracy The tense is right for the dates, with no changes inside a sentence
There are several complex sentences (with whereas, which, -ing or before -ing)
Most sentences have no errors
Subject-verb agreement is correct, especially with the number of and the percentage of

How to use the result. If you can tick most statements in all four groups, your answer is likely to be around Band 7. If one group has mostly empty boxes, work on that criterion first, using the module named in 16.4.

16.7 A six-week plan from Band 6 to Band 7

Week Focus Practice
1 The overview Write overviews only, for 10 visuals (Modules 13 and 15)
2 Selecting and grouping Plan 6 answers in 5 minutes each, with no writing
3 Tense and agreement Correction exercises (Module 15 C6), then 3 full answers
4 Vocabulary range Rewrite one answer, replacing every repeated verb
5 Complex sentences Add one whereas, one relative clause and one participle clause to 3 answers
6 Timed practice Four full answers in 20 minutes each, marked with the checklist
