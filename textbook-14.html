14.1 Describing every single number

Incorrect Corrected Why
In 2000, Northbury was 8%. In 2005 it was 11%. In 2010 it was 15%. In 2015 it was 22%. In 2020 it was 28%. Northbury rose steadily from 8% in 2000 to 28% in 2020, more than tripling. The incorrect version reads out every point and selects nothing. The correction gives the start, the end, the pattern and a ratio

How to avoid it. Report the start, the end and the peak or low point. Group items, and compare them.

14.2 Missing the overview

Incorrect Corrected Why
(Introduction) … In 2000, Eastfield had the highest percentage of cyclists, at 20% … (Introduction) … Overall, cycling became far more popular in Northbury, whereas it declined in Southport, and Eastfield ended close to where it began. In 2000, Eastfield had the highest percentage of cyclists, at 20% … With no overview, the examiner has to work out the main features. This usually limits Task Achievement to Band 5

How to avoid it. Plan the overview in your first five minutes, and write it directly after the introduction (see Module 13).

14.3 Giving personal opinions and reasons

Incorrect Corrected Why
It is surprising that social media became so popular, and I think this is bad for teenagers. Social media use quadrupled, from 4 to 16 hours a week. Task 1 reports information. It does not evaluate it
Visitor numbers rose because the weather was warmer. Visitor numbers rose as the temperature rose. The chart shows a link, not a cause. Because adds a reason that is not shown
Unfortunately, coal fell. The share of coal fell sharply to 18%. Words such as unfortunately and luckily are opinions

How to avoid it. Remove I think, surprising, unfortunately and every because that is not on the visual.

14.4 Repeating the question without paraphrasing

Incorrect Corrected Why
The chart shows the average number of hours per week that teenagers spent on five leisure activities in 2010 and 2025. The bar chart compares the average number of hours that teenagers spent each week on five leisure activities in 2010 and 2025. Words copied from the task are not counted as your own language. Change the verb, the phrase for time and the structure

How to avoid it. Use two techniques from Module 12 (for example a new verb and a new noun phrase) and keep every number and name.

14.5 Incorrect comparisons

Incorrect Corrected Why
Rent in Harbourton was higher than Stonebridge. Rent in Harbourton was higher than in Stonebridge. You must compare like with like: rent with rent, not rent with a city
Gas accounted for nearly twice as much as coal in 2000. Coal accounted for nearly twice as much as gas in 2000 (45% against 25%). The direction is wrong: coal was the larger one
Social media use tripled from 4 to 16 hours. Social media use quadrupled from 4 to 16 hours. 16 ÷ 4 = 4. Check every ratio
Eastfield's figure was 1% above its starting level. Eastfield's figure was one percentage point above its starting level. A difference between two percentages is in percentage points

How to avoid it. Do the arithmetic in the margin, write the larger figure first, and check that both sides of the comparison are the same kind of thing.

14.6 Incorrect tense usage

Incorrect Corrected Why
In 2010, the figure rises to 20% and then falls in 2015. In 2010, the figure rose to 20% and then fell in 2015. A past date needs the past simple
The figure will rise in 2015 (data from 2015 is shown). The figure rose in 2015. Do not use the future for dates that have passed
Sales have risen in 2010. Sales rose in 2010. The present perfect does not go with a finished past date
The olives were picked, and then they are washed. The olives are picked, and then they are washed. Keep one tense: a process takes the present simple passive
The chart showed the percentage of … The chart shows the percentage of … The chart exists now, even if its data is old

How to avoid it. Look at the dates first. Past dates take the past simple; no date or a process takes the present simple; future dates take will or is expected to.

14.7 Misusing vocabulary

Incorrect Corrected Why
The figure fluctuated from 5% to 9%. The figure rose from 5% to 9%. Fluctuate means to go up and down repeatedly
Sales jumped slightly. Sales rose slightly. Jump means a big, fast rise
The number of cars rose by 20% to 5%. The number of cars rose by 20% to 5,000. By gives the amount, to the end point, and a unit is needed
The amount of students increased. The number of students increased. Students are countable
The percent of women … The percentage of women … Percent is not a noun for a share
The graph peaked in 2010. The line peaked in 2010. A graph is not what peaks: the figure or the line does

How to avoid it. Use a word only when you know its exact meaning. A simple, correct word scores higher than a rare, wrong one (see Module 10).

14.8 Reporting inaccurate figures

Incorrect Corrected Why
Eastfield peaked at 30% in 2015. Eastfield peaked at 27% in 2010. The graph shows 27% in 2010 and 25% in 2015
Visitors rose to 70 million in June. Visitors rose to 70,000 in June. The axis shows thousands, so 70 means 70,000
Social media took 16% of leisure time. Social media took 16 hours a week. The unit is hours, not a percentage

How to avoid it. Put a finger on each figure and check it against the axis, the key and the unit. When a value is between gridlines, write approximately or just over.

14.9 Writing a conclusion instead of an overview

Incorrect Corrected Why
(Last paragraph) In conclusion, cycling is getting more popular, which is good for the environment. Delete it. Write an overview after the introduction: Overall, cycling became far more popular in Northbury, whereas it declined in Southport. An opinion is not allowed, and a conclusion at the end repeats instead of selecting

How to avoid it. Use the last minute of your 20 for checking, not for a conclusion. Do not write in conclusion in Task 1.

14.10 Using memorised phrases unnaturally

Incorrect Corrected Why
As we can see from the chart, the chart shows that the figures speak for themselves. Eastfield's figure climbed to a peak of 27% in 2010 before falling back to 21%. Empty memorised sentences waste words and say nothing
The figure skyrocketed dramatically to a zenith of 28%. The figure rose to a peak of 28%. Using several “strong” words together sounds unnatural
The line graph illustrates the number of … (the graph shows percentages) The line graph illustrates the percentage of … A memorised introduction must fit the data
In a nutshell, the table is very interesting. Overall, age had a strong influence on internet use. Informal idioms and opinions do not belong in Task 1

How to avoid it. Never memorise whole sentences. Learn flexible structures (Module 1.9) and put real data into them.

14.11 Writing fewer than 150 words

An answer under 150 words is penalised in Task Achievement, because it cannot cover the main features and the comparisons properly. Counting your words during the test costs time, so aim for 170 to 190 and count only if you are unsure.

An answer that is too short (105 words).

The bar chart compares the average number of hours that teenagers spent each week on five leisure activities in 2010 and 2025. Overall, social media went up and television went down. In 2010, television was the highest at 14 hours, followed by sport at 8 hours and reading at 6 hours. Video games were at 5 hours and social media was the lowest at 4 hours. In 2025, social media was the highest at 16 hours. Television was 7 hours. Video games rose from 5 hours to 9 hours. Reading fell from 6 hours to 3 hours. Sport changed from 8 hours to 7 hours.

How to add about 50 words that are worth marks (the four additions below bring the answer to about 156 words):

Add Example
A ratio Social media use quadrupled, while television use halved.
The exception Sport and exercise was the most stable activity, dipping only slightly from 8 to 7 hours.
A stronger overview Replace the first overview with: Overall, teenagers spent much more time on social media and video games in 2025, whereas television and reading both declined sharply.
A change in ranking Social media, the least popular activity in 2010, had become the most time-consuming by 2025.

How to avoid it. Write two full body paragraphs of about 65 to 75 words each. Add comparisons (ratios, rankings, exceptions), not repetition, to reach the length.
