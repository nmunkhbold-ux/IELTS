Module 8: Advantages and disadvantages essays

These essays look easy, because students can always list some good points and some bad points. The difficulty is the judgement. When the question says outweigh, the examiner expects you to decide which side is heavier and to prove it. This module shows how to weigh, not just list.

A. Understanding the question

Wording Main parts What the examiner expects
What are the advantages and disadvantages of this? 1. Advantages 2. Disadvantages Both sides developed. A short overall view in the conclusion is wise
Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages? 1. Advantages 2. Disadvantages 3. Your judgement A clear decision on which side is greater, with reasons
Do you think the benefits are greater than the drawbacks? The same as outweigh The same
Is this a positive or negative development? Treated as a two-part question See Module 9

What "outweigh" means. One side is heavier than the other. You must say which side, and why. Writing both sides are equal is risky, because it looks as if you have no position.

Balance. Both sides need real development. A paragraph of four sentences for advantages and a paragraph of two for disadvantages leaves a main part only partly answered.

B. Planning the answer

Three plans

Plan Body 1 Body 2 Use when
1. Two blocks Advantages (two) Disadvantages (two) What are the advantages and disadvantages?
2. Weaker side first (for "outweigh") The side you think is weaker, fully explained The stronger side, with the reasons it is heavier. The last paragraph carries your argument Do the advantages outweigh...?
3. Point by point One topic, with its advantage and its disadvantage (cost, for example) A second topic in the same way Only when ideas come in natural pairs. It is harder to control

Plan 2 is the strongest for Band 7. It ends the argument on the side you support, so the reader's last impression is the one you want.

How to weigh. Compare the two sides with these four questions:

Question Heavier side
Who is affected? The side that affects more people
How serious is it? The side with greater harm or benefit
How long does it last? The side that lasts longer
Can it be fixed? A disadvantage that can be reduced weighs less

In the model essay, the disadvantages (shop closures, fraud, returns) can mostly be reduced, while the advantages (convenience, savings, access) affect almost every customer every day. That is why the advantages weigh more.

A completed plan (Plan 2)

An increasing number of people choose to buy goods online instead of in shops. Do the advantages of this trend outweigh the disadvantages?

Notes
Question T: online shopping. F: whether benefits are greater than drawbacks. I: outweigh, 3 parts. L: an increasing number
Judgement Advantages outweigh
Introduction Paraphrase. Position: advantages greater overall, despite some drawbacks
Body 1: advantages Convenience (time and travel saved; example: parent, elderly person). Price comparison. Access for small towns
Body 2: disadvantages + weighing Small shops close; cannot touch goods; returns; payment fraud. Judgement: these can be reduced (local shops sell online; secure payments; return policies)
Conclusion Benefits for customers are greater. Drawbacks exist but can be reduced

C. Writing structure

Paragraph Content Frames
Introduction Topic, then your position or an outline Although this trend has some drawbacks, I believe that the advantages are greater overall.
Body 1 Main advantage (or disadvantage) with explanation and example, then one or two further points The main advantage of... is... / A further benefit is... / In addition,...
Body 2 The other side, then your weighing On the other hand, there are real disadvantages. / However, I believe that these problems are less serious than...
Conclusion Both sides in brief, then the judgement In conclusion,... Although..., I believe that the advantages outweigh the disadvantages.

Vocabulary for advantages and disadvantages

Instead of Use
good point advantage, benefit, strength, positive aspect
bad point disadvantage, drawback, downside, weakness, negative aspect
good for beneficial to, useful for, helpful for
bad for harmful to, damaging to, a risk to
more good than bad the benefits outweigh the drawbacks, the advantages are greater overall

Phrases for weighing

Despite these drawbacks,... / Although these problems are real,... / On balance,... / Overall,... / The disadvantages are less serious because they can be reduced.

Common mistakes in advantages and disadvantages essays

Mistake Fix
A list of four points on each side, with no development Two developed points on each side
No judgement for an outweigh question Say which side is heavier and why
Mixing advantages and disadvantages in one paragraph Keep each side in its own paragraph (Plan 1 or 2)
Using pros and cons Use advantages and disadvantages in a formal essay
Weighing with feelings (I think it is better) Weigh with reasons (who, how serious, can be fixed)
A disadvantage that is really an advantage in disguise Check that each point is really on the right side

D. Band 7+ model essay

An increasing number of people choose to buy goods online instead of in shops. Do the advantages of this trend outweigh the disadvantages?

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

Write at least 250 words.

Model answer · 280 words

Shopping on the internet has grown rapidly in recent years, and many consumers now prefer it to visiting traditional shops. Although this trend has some drawbacks, particularly for local businesses, I believe that the advantages are greater overall.

The main advantage of online shopping is convenience. Customers can order almost anything at any time of day, without spending time and money on travel. A parent with small children, or an elderly person who cannot walk far, for example, can have food and medicine delivered to the door. Prices are also easier to compare online, so shoppers can find the best offer within a few minutes and save money. In addition, people in small towns and villages can buy goods that were never available in their local shops.

On the other hand, there are real disadvantages. Small shops cannot always compete with large websites, and when they close, town centres lose jobs and activity. Customers also cannot see or touch products before buying them, which sometimes leads to disappointment, and returning goods can be inconvenient. Some buyers also feel that they miss the social side of visiting a market or a shopping centre. Moreover, online payments carry a risk of fraud. However, I believe that these problems are less serious than the benefits. Local shops can start to sell online themselves, and secure payment systems and clear return policies are becoming more common, so most of these risks can be reduced.

In conclusion, online shopping saves time, money and effort, and it gives more people access to the goods they need. Although it creates difficulties for local shops and some risks for customers, I believe that the advantages outweigh the disadvantages.

E. The model essay explained

Paragraph by paragraph

Paragraph What it does Why it works
1. Introduction (38 words) Paraphrases the topic and gives the judgement at once (the advantages are greater overall) The examiner knows the position from the start, and the word although signals that both sides will appear
2. Body 1: advantages (89 words) Topic sentence: convenience. Explanation, example (parent, elderly person), then price comparison and access for small towns Each advantage is explained, and the example is realistic
3. Body 2: disadvantages and weighing (113 words) Four disadvantages in brief, then the judgement: these problems are less serious than the benefits, and a reason (they can be reduced) Plan 2: the weighing is in the last body paragraph. The reasons for the weighing (can be reduced) are specific
4. Conclusion (40 words) Both sides in brief, then the judgement in the question's own word (outweigh) No new ideas. The position matches the introduction

The four criteria

Criterion Evidence in the essay What would push it further
Task Response All three main parts are answered. Both sides are developed. The judgement is clear and supported by a reason A specific example for the disadvantage side (a named kind of shop)
Coherence and Cohesion Clear signals: The main advantage..., On the other hand..., However..., Moreover..., In conclusion.... Reference words: this trend, these problems, these risks Fewer sentences beginning with a signal word
Lexical Resource drawbacks, convenience, compare prices, compete with, carry a risk of fraud, secure payment systems, return policies A few more collocations on the disadvantage side
Grammatical Range and Accuracy Relative clauses (who cannot walk far, which sometimes leads to). Concession (Although this trend has...). Modals (can order, can be reduced). Comparatives (easier, less serious than). Passive (can be reduced) More conditional and participle structures (Module 12)

F. Try it yourself

Many people now choose to live and work in a foreign country for a few years. Do the advantages of this outweigh the disadvantages?

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

Write at least 250 words.

  1. Analyse (3 minutes). T, F, I (3 parts), L.
  2. Plan (5 minutes). List two advantages and two disadvantages. Use the four weighing questions to decide which side is heavier. Choose Plan 2.
  3. Write (27 minutes) and check (5 minutes).
  4. Compare with the sample plan.
Show the sample plan

Your plan may differ and still be good.

  • Analysis. T: living abroad. F: whether the advantages are greater than the disadvantages. I: outweigh, 3 parts. L: a few years.
  • Judgement. Advantages outweigh.
  • Body 1 (disadvantages first, the weaker side). Homesickness and loneliness, and distance from family (example: missing a relative's wedding or illness). Difficulty with language and culture at first.
  • Body 2 (advantages, with weighing). New skills and a wider view of the world (example: a worker who learns a new language and gains international experience, which helps his career). Higher pay or better opportunities in some countries. Weighing: the disadvantages are temporary and can be reduced by calling home and joining local groups, but the experience lasts for life.
  • Conclusion. Difficulties are real but short-term; the benefits last.

More advantages and disadvantages questions for practice

  1. More people are choosing to work from home. What are the advantages and disadvantages?
  2. Some cities have introduced a charge for drivers who enter the city centre. Do the advantages of this outweigh the disadvantages?
  3. Many students now take courses online rather than in classrooms. What are the advantages and disadvantages?
  4. Tourism is the main source of income in some countries. Do the advantages of this outweigh the disadvantages?
  5. Some people prefer to live in a house with a garden, while others prefer an apartment. What are the advantages and disadvantages of living in an apartment?
  6. Young children now learn to use computers at school. Do the advantages of this outweigh the disadvantages?
