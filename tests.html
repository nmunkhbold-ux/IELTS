Practice tests

Full Reading tests, real exam format.

Each test has 3 passages, 40 questions and 60 minutes, in the same layout as the computer-delivered IELTS. Your answers are saved as you go, so you can come back to a test later.

⏱60 minutesA countdown timer; the test submits itself when time runs out.
✦Highlight and noteSelect any words in the passage to highlight them or add a note.
⚑ReviewFlag questions to come back to, and see unanswered ones before you submit.
✓Instant markingYour score, an estimated band and every answer explained.
Writing practice

Writing tests, real exam screen.

Each test has Task 1 (a chart, process or map) and Task 2 (an essay) in one 60-minute session, with a live word count. Your writing is saved as you type. When you finish, you send it to your teacher by email.
