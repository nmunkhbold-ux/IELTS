Writing Task 1 textbook

This textbook takes pre-intermediate (B1) students to Band 7+ in IELTS Academic Writing Task 1, one visual type at a time. It has 17 modules, and every graph, model answer and exercise answer was checked against its data.

How each module works

Every visual-type module uses the same five parts: A Understanding the question · B Analysing the visual · C Writing structure · D Band 7+ model answer with its visual · E Model answer explained. All charts and data are original and fictional. The model answers show a method; students should never memorise them.

A four-step routine for teaching any module

  1. Students study the visual and complete the five-look method (Module 1) before reading anything.
  2. Read sections A–C together and fix the structure.
  3. Students write their own answer in 20 minutes before they see the model.
  4. Compare with section D, then work through the annotations in section E.

Using this textbook

Start with Module 1, then work through Modules 2 to 9 in the order that suits your students, using the four-step routine above. Use Modules 10 to 14 as reference and correction material while students write, Module 15 for practice, Module 16 for improving a weaker answer, and Module 17 for revision and the mock test. Every module can also be taught on its own.
