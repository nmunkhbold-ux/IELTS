Module 15: From Band 5.5 or 6 to Band 7+

Many students get stuck at 5.5 or 6 for months. They are not weak: they have a structure, they know the topic and their English is understandable. But the same habits keep their score down in all four criteria. This module shows what those habits look like in a real essay, and gives you a way to fix them one by one.

A. Six habits that keep an essay at Band 6

# Habit Criterion it affects The fix
1 A position that appears only at the end, or is unclear Task Response State the position in the introduction. Repeat it in the conclusion
2 Ideas listed, not developed (four points, no examples) Task Response Two ideas, each with explanation and example (Module 3, Module 13)
3 The same linkers at the start of every sentence Coherence and Cohesion Join sentences. Use reference words (Module 12)
4 Simple, repeated vocabulary (good, bad, big, a lot of) Lexical Resource Replace with precise words and collocations (Module 11)
5 Mostly simple sentences, with errors in the complex ones Grammatical Range and Accuracy Practise ten complex patterns (Module 12)
6 The same small errors repeated (articles, agreement, word forms) Lexical Resource and Grammar Keep an error log (part D below)

B. The same question, two essays

Here is an original Band 5.5 to 6 essay on the question that the model in Module 8 answers. Read it first. Then compare it with the Band 7+ model essay in Module 8.

An increasing number of people choose to buy goods online instead of in shops. Do the advantages of this trend outweigh the disadvantages?

Band 5.5–6 answer (252 words)

Nowadays, shopping online is becoming more popular. Many people buy things from internet shops every day. Some people think the advantages outweigh the disadvantages. In this essay I will discuss both sides and give my opinion.

Firstly, online shopping is very convenient. People can buy everything at home and they do not need go to the shops. Moreover, it save a lot of time. Secondly, the prices on internet is often cheaper than in shops. Furthermore, there are many choices and people can compare. Customers can read reviews and choose the best one. Also, there are special offers on many websites. In addition, you can shop 24 hours. People who live far from shops can save money on transport. This is a big advantage for people who are busy.

However, there are also some disadvantages. Firstly, people can not see the products before they buy. They may be bad quality. Secondly, there is a risk about fraud and personal information. Online shops can also keep the personal details of customers. Another problem is that sometimes the goods arrive late or they are the wrong thing. Furthermore, small shops will close because they have not customers, and this is bad for the economy. Also, many people feel lonely because they not meet other people when shopping.

In conclusion, online shopping has advantages and disadvantages. I think the advantages are more than the disadvantages, but we should be careful. Online shopping is the future, and many people will use it in the next years.

Teaching estimate: about Band 5.5. Task Response 6, Coherence and Cohesion 5.5, Lexical Resource 5.5, Grammatical Range and Accuracy 5. The average is 5.5.

Why this essay stays at about 5.5

Criterion What this essay shows What the Band 7+ model (Module 8) shows
Task Response All the parts are answered, but the position is weak (I think the advantages are more than..., only in the conclusion). Eight points are listed with no example for any of them. There is no weighing of the disadvantages The position is clear in paragraph 1. Each paragraph has a developed idea with an example (a parent, an elderly person). The weighing is explicit (these problems are less serious than the benefits) with a reason
Coherence and Cohesion Paragraphs are clear. But twelve of the sentences start with a linker, for example (Firstly, Moreover, Secondly, Furthermore, Also, In addition, However, Furthermore, Also). Almost no reference words Sentences are joined (which, so, although). Reference words point back (this trend, these problems, these risks). Linkers are varied and not at the start of every sentence
Lexical Resource Vocabulary is simple and repeated: big, bad, good, a lot of. Collocation errors: a risk about fraud, have not customers, the wrong thing. You is used (informal) Precise collocations: carry a risk of fraud, compete with, secure payment systems, return policies, compare prices
Grammatical Range and Accuracy Mostly simple sentences. Repeated errors: do not need go, it save, the prices... is, can not, they not meet, have not customers, no article in on internet A range of complex sentences (relative clauses, concession, passive, modals). Almost all sentences are error-free

Sentence-level rewrites. The best way to see the difference is to rewrite sentence by sentence.

Band 5.5 sentence The problem Band 7 version
Nowadays, shopping online is becoming more popular. A memorised opening with a plain structure Shopping on the internet has grown rapidly in recent years.
In this essay I will discuss both sides and give my opinion. Announces the essay but has no position Although this trend has some drawbacks, I believe that the advantages are greater overall.
People can buy everything at home and they do not need go to the shops. Missing to, and everything is too general Customers can order almost anything without travelling to the shops.
Moreover, it save a lot of time. Agreement error, vague This also saves time and money on travel.
The prices on internet is often cheaper than in shops. Article and agreement errors Prices on the internet are often lower than in shops.
In addition, you can shop 24 hours. You is informal. For is missing Customers can also shop at any time of day.
This is a big advantage for people who are busy. Big is vague. No example This is particularly valuable for parents with small children and for elderly people who cannot walk far.
There is a risk about fraud and personal information. Wrong collocation Online payments carry a risk of fraud.
Small shops will close because they have not customers. Grammar error. No explanation Small shops cannot always compete with large websites, and when they close, town centres lose jobs and activity.
I think the advantages are more than the disadvantages. Incorrect phrase and comparative I believe that the advantages outweigh the disadvantages.

C. How to mark your own essay

Use these seven steps. They take about ten minutes. The bands you give yourself are estimates, so ask a teacher to check one essay in every four.

  1. Read once for meaning. Can you state the writer's position in one sentence? If you have to hunt for it, Task Response is at about Band 5 or lower.
  2. Run the limit checks (Module 1): Is a main part only partly answered? Is paragraphing missing? Is the format wrong? Is the vocabulary unrelated to the task? Are almost all sentences simple?
  3. Task Response. List the main parts. Tick each one. Count the main ideas that have an explanation and an example.
  4. Coherence and Cohesion. Write a five-word topic for each paragraph. Count the sentences that begin with a linker. Check that each pronoun points to something.
  5. Lexical Resource. Underline the collocations. Underline the word errors. How many times do you repeat the same word?
  6. Grammatical Range and Accuracy. In two paragraphs, count the sentences and the error-free sentences. Note any repeated error.
  7. Average the four scores.
Band 5.5 Band 6 Band 7
Task Response One main part is weak or thin. The position is unclear. Ideas are listed All main parts are answered. The position is relevant. Some ideas are not developed All parts are answered fully. The position is clear throughout. Most ideas have explanations and examples
Coherence and Cohesion Some order, but the linking is repeated or inaccurate Clear overall order. Linking sometimes mechanical Clear progression throughout. A range of linking. One topic per paragraph
Lexical Resource Simple words repeated. Errors in word choice and spelling are noticeable Adequate range. Some errors with less common words Enough range for some flexibility. Some collocations. A few errors
Grammatical Range and Accuracy Mostly simple sentences. Errors are frequent but meaning is usually clear Simple and complex sentences. Errors occur in complex ones A variety of complex structures. Many error-free sentences

D. The error log and fourteen common errors

Copy this table into a notebook. After every essay, add each error you made: write the wrong version, the correct version, and the rule. Before every timed essay, read the last ten rows. Writers who keep an error log usually cut their repeated errors by half within a month.

Date Wrong Correct Rule
the prices is cheaper prices are cheaper The verb agrees with the plural subject

Fourteen errors that appear in many Band 5.5 essays

# Not this Write this Rule
1 many peoples, informations, advices many people, information, advice People is already plural. Information and advice are uncountable
2 the pollution is bad (in general) pollution is harmful No article for general uncountable nouns
3 on internet, in the society on the internet, in society Learn fixed patterns
4 he don't, it save, the prices is he does not, it saves, the prices are Check subject–verb agreement in every sentence
5 do not need go, should builds do not need to go, should build A modal verb is followed by the base verb (no to, no -s)
6 people can not, they not meet people cannot, they do not meet One word cannot. Use do not with a verb
7 a risk about, discuss about, depend of a risk of, discuss, depend on Learn the preposition with each word
8 more cheaper, more better cheaper, better Do not combine more with -er
9 In my opinion, I think In my opinion,... or I think... Do not say it twice
10 In the other hand On the other hand A fixed phrase
11 Cars are useful, they cause pollution. Cars are useful, but they cause pollution. Do not join two sentences with only a comma
12 Because it saves time. (alone) Many people shop online because it saves time. A because clause cannot stand alone
13 kids, a lot of, you can children, many, people can Use formal words and avoid addressing the reader
14 The advantage is more than the disadvantage The advantages outweigh the disadvantages Learn outweigh

E. A six-week improvement plan

This plan is for students at about Band 5.5 to 6 who are working alone. It needs about four hours a week.

1 Read every question correctly Modules 1 and 2 Analyse ten questions with TFIL. Write one introduction a day Did I find all the main parts?

F. Try it yourself

Exercise 1: rewrite each sentence from the Band 5.5 essay in a more precise, error-free form.

  1. Also, there are special offers on many websites.
  2. They may be bad quality.
  3. Another problem is that sometimes the goods arrive late or they are the wrong thing.
  4. Also, many people feel lonely because they not meet other people when shopping.
  5. Online shopping is the future, and many people will use it in the next years.
Show the answer key

Answers vary. Models:

  1. Many websites also offer special discounts that are not available in shops.
  2. Some goods may be of poor quality.
  3. Another problem is that goods sometimes arrive late or are not what the customer ordered.
  4. Some customers also feel lonely, because they no longer meet other people while shopping.
  5. Online shopping is likely to become even more common over the next few years. (or: remove the sentence, because it adds no support)

Exercise 2: mark the Band 5.5 essay yourself. Use the seven steps in part C. Give a band for each criterion, write one piece of evidence for each, and write one sentence on what the essay needs to reach the next band. Compare with the teaching estimate in part B.

Exercise 3: rewrite paragraph 3. Rewrite the disadvantages paragraph (However, there are also some disadvantages...) in about 100 words. Use one clear idea about small shops, one about trust, a counter-argument, and one linking sentence that weighs the two sides. Then compare it with paragraph 3 of the model essay in Module 8.

Exercise 4: mark your own starting-point essay. Take the essay you wrote at the end of Module 1 and mark it with the seven steps. Choose the single habit from part A that most limits your score and make it your target for the next three essays.
